## 📐 Page Structure

**"Get notified when the AI that plans your life launches"**

- Alternative: "AI that plans your life — daily, weekly, automatically"
- Clear benefit-driven messaging
- Immediately tells visitors what the product does

"Elyxa adapts your routines, goals, and energy so you finally stay on track — no weekly resets."

- Addresses pain point (inconsistency)

- **Input Field Label:** "Your email for early access"
- **Placeholder:** "jane@example.com"
- **Button Text:** "Join Early Access"
- **Button Color:** High contrast (blue/purple recommended)

#### Trust Text (Under Button):

"No spam — early invites & updates only."

- Reduces friction
- Builds trust
- Clear expectations

## 🎨 Design Principles

### Clarity Above All

- Visitors should understand immediately what Elyxa does
- If confused, they won't sign up
- Use simple, direct language
- Avoid jargon

### Make CTA Stand Out

- High contrast button color
- Large, tappable on mobile
- Clear action verb ("Join", "Get", "Reserve")
- Repeat CTA if page is long

### Mobile-First Layout

- Most traffic will be mobile
- Text must be readable without zooming
- Buttons must be easy to tap (min 44px)
- Forms must be thumb-friendly
- Test on actual devices

### Visual Hierarchy

- Most important info at top
- Progressive information disclosure
- Whitespace for breathing room
- Logical flow from problem → solution → action

## 📝 Copywriting Guidelines

### Headline Best Practices

- Lead with benefit, not feature
- Use clear, simple language
- Create curiosity
- Make a promise
- Keep it under 10 words if possible

### Sub-Headline Purpose

- Expand on the headline
- Address objections
- Build desire
- Clarify the offer

### Button Text Options

- "Join Early Access" ✅ (Recommended)
- "Get Notified"
- "Reserve My Spot"
- "Join the Waitlist"
- "Count Me In"

Avoid:

- "Submit"
- "Enter"
- "Sign Up" (feels like more commitment)
