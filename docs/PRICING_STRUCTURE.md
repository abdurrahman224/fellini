## Subscription Logic

### Trial Period:

- 7-day free trial (optional)
- Full feature access during trial
- Auto-convert to paid at trial end
- Easy cancellation before charge

### Billing Cycles:

- Monthly: Charged monthly on signup date
- Annual: Charged yearly on signup date
- Automatic renewal
- Email reminder before renewal

### Upgrades:

- Immediate access to new tier
- Prorated credit applied
- New price at next billing

### Downgrades:

- Takes effect at next billing cycle
- Keep current features until then
- Confirmation email sent

### Cancellations:

- Access until end of billing period
- No partial refunds (unless policy allows)
- Data retained for 30 days
- Re-activation option

### Failed Payments:

- 3 retry attempts
- Email notification on each attempt
- Grace period: 7 days
- Account suspension after grace period
- Reactivation on successful payment
